Capsule Allocator

Splits a long-hold capsule budget using each capsule's price trend on Steam and how fast its stickers are being used up. Prices are editable; the split updates as you type.

How to split the budget

Capsule Last 12 months Price each 30 d 90 d Days of supply Trend Scarcity Share of budget Buy Cost Break-even

"Buy" rounds to whole capsules, then spends what is left on the capsule furthest below its target. Break-even is the Steam listing price at which a sale gives back what you paid, after Steam's 15% fee.

How each weight is built

Weight = tilt × trend × scarcity, then each capsule gets its weight's share of the budget.

Tilt is the old rule: Gentle gives 1/√price, Strong gives 1/price, Equal € gives every capsule the same money.

Trend compares the last Steam sale with a reference price, as a log ratio so a rise and a fall of the same size count the same. Buy the dip uses the median price of the past year (since release for younger capsules). Follow momentum uses the price 90 days ago. Dip with brake takes the dip reading and removes any fall of the last 30 days.

Scarcity is the consumption pace from the Major Sticker Scarcity study, per capsule: the stickers of that capsule on sale today on 9 markets plus its sealed capsules on sale, divided by how many of its stickers were applied to guns per day over the last 30 days. Fewer days of supply means the market is being used up faster. Each capsule is compared with the median of days. Rio 2022 has no public application data, so its three capsules sit at the median (×1.00).

Each factor is estrength × score, kept between ×0.33 and ×3 so one signal can never wipe out or swamp a capsule. Strength 0 switches a factor off; both at 0 with Gentle gives the old Capsule Split result.
