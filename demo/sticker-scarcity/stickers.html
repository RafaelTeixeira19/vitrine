Stickers

Reading: medians and percentiles are computed over every sticker of the finish that has application data, all Majors together, whatever the filters; filters only choose what is shown. "Applied / supply" is the number of stickers applied for each one on sale today; its percentile ranks it among the same finish.
Applied: stickers currently applied to guns, from cs2stickertracker.com (sum of its 1x to 5x buckets; guns = 1x). It rounds large counts to the thousand, which makes the per-sticker 30-day pace noisy for popular Paper stickers. Stockholm 2021 and Rio 2022 have no application source.
Supply: listings open today on Steam, CSFloat, BUFF, YouPin, C5GAME, HaloSkins, Skinport, Waxpeer and Market.CSGO, the same sources as the Major page. Untick markets you think list the same items twice.
Sealed capsules: each sticker gets the listed capsules that contain it, times the odds of its rarity (80.13% High Grade, 16.03% Remarkable, 3.21% Exotic, 0.64% Extraordinary), divided by the number of stickers of that rarity in the capsule, organiser stickers included.
Lowest price: the cheapest of Steam (with Steam's fees), CSFloat and Skinport, in US dollars.
