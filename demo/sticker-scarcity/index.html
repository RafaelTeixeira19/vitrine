Major × variant

Applied: stickers currently applied to guns, from cs2stickertracker.com. Its 1x to 5x figures count guns with at least 1, 2... 5 copies, so their sum is the number of stickers applied (the site's own "weighted sticker slots") and 1x is the number of guns. The site says it is in early development, and rounds large counts to the thousand. A sticker scraped off a gun leaves the count, so this is a floor of what was consumed, and a 30-day figure can dip below zero.
Stockholm 2021 and Rio 2022: no public source counts their applications. The tracker does not cover them, CSFloat's FloatDB needs an account, and Steam's description search only finds guns on sale, with a ratio to the tracker that swings by a factor of five. They appear in the tables with their supply, not on the charts.
Supply: listings open today, by market. Steam comes from the Steam market itself; BUFF, YouPin, C5GAME and HaloSkins from SteamDT; CSFloat, Skinport, Waxpeer and Market.CSGO from their own public price feeds. CSFloat, Skinport and Waxpeer match the counts csgoskins.gg shows; Market.CSGO could not be cross-checked and its counts move a lot within a day. These nine markets cover every sticker and capsule alike, which keeps Majors comparable. The same item can be listed on several markets at once, so ticking them all gives an upper bound. Left out: CS.MONEY (its market API refuses automated browsers; about 1% of sticker offers on the 150 items where csgoskins.gg showed every market) and the other markets only csgoskins.gg lists, such as csboard and NTSkins (it blocks automated reading after about 150 pages). Nobody publishes how many stickers sit in inventories.
Sealed capsules: listed team capsules count as stickers not yet opened. Each team sticker gets the listed capsules that contain it, times the unboxing odds of its rarity (80.13% High Grade, 16.03% Remarkable, 3.21% Exotic, 0.64% Extraordinary), divided by the number of stickers of that rarity in the capsule. Organiser stickers (BLAST.tv, PGL...) share the odds but are not counted, so a capsule is worth a little less than one team sticker. Which finish holds which rarity changes between Majors.
30-day pace: change in stickers applied between the two tracker snapshots closest to 30 days apart, scaled to 30 days.
Cologne 2026 broke the old supply model: no capsules and no $0.25 clearance. Stickers were bought one by one in the in-game Major Hub at demand-based prices until 29 September 2026. Its second "Ranked Series" (fixed prices, from 29 July) is not tracked for applications and is left out.
